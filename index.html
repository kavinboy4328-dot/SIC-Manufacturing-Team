About SIC Manufacturing Team

The SIC Manufacturing Team is a student-driven team focused on manufacturing technologies, industrial learning, innovation and practical engineering applications.


Our goal is to connect classroom knowledge with real-world manufacturing industries through industry visits, technical activities, projects and innovation.

About SIC Manufacturing Team

The SIC Manufacturing Team is a student-driven initiative of the Department of Mechanical Engineering at Sri Ramakrishna Institute of Technology.

Our team focuses on practical manufacturing knowledge, industrial exposure, innovation and project-based learning.

Our goal is to connect classroom learning with real-world manufacturing industries.

Andal Engineering Works

Industry Visit: 01

Industry Type: Metal Casting / Foundry

Location: Selvaraj Puram, Kannampalayam, Singanallur

Visit Focus: Casting and Foundry Manufacturing Processes

About the Industry Visit

The SIC Manufacturing Team visited Andal Engineering Works as part of industrial exposure and practical learning activities. The visit helped students understand the practical application of manufacturing and metal casting concepts studied in Mechanical Engineering.

Casting Processes and Activities

  • Casting Terminology
  • Pattern Preparation
  • Green Sand Moulding
  • Mould Preparation
  • Core Preparation
  • Metal Melting
  • Molten Metal Handling
  • Pouring Process
  • Solidification and Cooling
  • Gating System
  • Riser System
  • Shakeout
  • Fettling
  • Cleaning and Finishing
  • Inspection and Quality Checking

Learning Outcomes

  • Understanding practical casting operations
  • Understanding moulds and cores
  • Understanding green sand moulding
  • Understanding gating and riser systems
  • Understanding solidification and cooling
  • Understanding casting defects
  • Understanding foundry workflow
  • Understanding industrial safety practices
  • Connecting classroom theory with industry practice

Relevance to Mechanical Engineering

The industry visit provided practical exposure to metal casting and foundry operations. It helped students connect theoretical knowledge with actual manufacturing practices.

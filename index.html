About SIC Manufacturing Team

The SIC Manufacturing Team is a student-driven initiative of the Department of Mechanical Engineering at Sri Ramakrishna Institute of Technology.

Our team focuses on practical manufacturing knowledge, industrial exposure, innovation and project-based learning.

Our goal is to connect classroom learning with real-world manufacturing industries.

About the Industry

Andal Engineering Works was visited as part of the SIC Manufacturing Team's industrial exposure activities. The visit provided students with practical exposure to casting and foundry manufacturing processes.

Metal Casting Processes Observed

  • Casting terminology
  • Pattern preparation
  • Green sand moulding
  • Mould preparation
  • Core preparation
  • Metal melting
  • Molten metal handling
  • Pouring
  • Solidification and cooling
  • Gating system
  • Riser system
  • Shakeout
  • Fettling
  • Cleaning and finishing
  • Inspection and quality checking

Learning Outcomes

  • Understanding practical casting operations
  • Understanding moulds and cores
  • Understanding green sand moulding
  • Understanding gating and riser systems
  • Understanding solidification and cooling
  • Identifying casting defects
  • Understanding foundry safety and workflow
  • Connecting classroom theory with industry practice

Relevance to Mechanical Engineering

The visit helped students understand how Metal Casting concepts studied in Mechanical Engineering are applied in real manufacturing and foundry environments.

About SIC Manufacturing Team

The SIC Manufacturing Team is a student-driven initiative of the Department of Mechanical Engineering at Sri Ramakrishna Institute of Technology.

Our team focuses on practical manufacturing knowledge, industrial exposure, innovation and project-based learning.

Our goal is to connect classroom learning with real-world manufacturing industries.

Manufacturing Technology

Metal Casting

Casting processes, moulds, cores, gating systems, risers and casting defects.

METAL CASTING

Casting Process

Terminology, green sand molding, investment casting, die casting, patterns, molds and cores, solidification and cooling, riser and gating systems, and casting defects.

JOINING PROCESSES

Gas welding: flame types and equipment; Electric Arc Welding (EAW), Gas Metal Arc Welding (GMAW), Gas Tungsten Arc Welding (GTAW); resistance welding: spot, seam, projection and flash; welding defects; laser beam and friction-stir welding; principles of brazing, soldering and adhesive bonding.

SHEET METAL PROCESSES

Principle of shearing, punch-die clearance; sheet metal operations including blanking, punching, bending, drawing and spinning; blanking force calculation; die-set: simple, progressive and compound; press: open back inclinable press and hydraulic press; deep drawing and bending processes.

BULK DEFORMATION PROCESSES

Plastic deformation, hot and cold working; rolling – rolling mills and defects in rolled parts; drawing – wire and rod drawing; forging – open and closed die forging, forging hammers and rotary swaging; extrusion – direct and indirect extrusion.

POWDER METALLURGY AND PLASTIC PROCESSING

Production of metal powder: atomization and crushing; blending; compacting; die pressing and isostatic pressing; sintering; types and characteristics of plastics; molding of thermoplastics and working principles.

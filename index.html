About SIC Manufacturing Team

The SIC Manufacturing Team is a student-driven initiative of the Department of Mechanical Engineering at Sri Ramakrishna Institute of Technology.

Our team focuses on practical manufacturing knowledge, industrial exposure, innovation and project-based learning.

Our goal is to connect classroom learning with real-world manufacturing industries.

Andal Engineering Works

Visit: Industry Visit 01

Location: Selvaraj Puram, Kannampalayam, Singanallur

Industry: Metal Casting / Foundry

Purpose: To understand practical foundry and metal casting operations.

Andal Engineering Works – Industry Visit 01

About the Visit

The industrial visit provided practical exposure to metal casting and foundry-related manufacturing activities. Students could observe the relationship between classroom concepts and real industrial practices.

Relevant Casting Topics

  • Foundry and casting process
  • Pattern and mould preparation
  • Sand moulding
  • Gating and risering
  • Melting and pouring
  • Solidification and cooling
  • Cleaning and finishing of cast components
  • Common casting defects and inspection

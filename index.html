Our team focuses on manufacturing knowledge, industrial exposure, innovation, practical engineering and project-based learning.

Our goal is to connect classroom knowledge with real-world manufacturing industries.

CNC Machining

CNC machining uses computer-controlled machines to manufacture components with high accuracy.

Applications include turning, milling, drilling and precision component manufacturing.

Welding

Welding is a joining process used to permanently join two or more materials.

Common processes include Arc Welding, MIG Welding and TIG Welding.

Casting

Casting is a manufacturing process in which molten material is poured into a mould and allowed to solidify.

It is widely used for producing complex-shaped mechanical components.

3D Printing

3D printing is an additive manufacturing process that creates components layer by layer.

It is widely used for rapid prototyping, product development and customized components.

CAD / CAM

CAD is used for computer-aided product design, while CAM helps in computer-aided manufacturing.

CAD/CAM improves design accuracy and manufacturing efficiency.

Manufacturing Automation

Manufacturing automation uses machines, control systems and robotics to perform manufacturing operations.

Automation can improve productivity, consistency and process efficiency.
